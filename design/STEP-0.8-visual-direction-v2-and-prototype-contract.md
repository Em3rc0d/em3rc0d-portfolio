# STEP 0.8 — THE BUILD ROOM · Visual Direction v2 & Prototype Contract

**Status:** active / pre-build visual gate  
**Supersedes:** the first Figma static exploration as final direction  
**Does not supersede:** STEP 0.7 visual-system principles

## 1. Why v2 exists

The first static Figma direction was structurally correct but visually too close to a polished generic tech/startup portfolio:

- giant sans-serif headline;
- dark background;
- small monospace metadata;
- capability cards;
- restrained red accents;
- conventional section stacking.

The problem was not the information architecture.

The problem was that motion was being added **after** composition instead of determining the composition.

Therefore STEP 0.8 changes the design problem from:

> “How do we animate a beautiful page?”

into:

> **“How does the system transform as the visitor moves through it?”**

## 2. Visual Direction v2 thesis

THE BUILD ROOM should behave more like an **interactive engineering editorial environment** than a startup landing page.

The visitor should not feel that they are scrolling through cards.

They should feel that they are:

```text
entering
activating
tracing
opening
inspecting
verifying
```

## 3. Startup → Home transformation

The startup is not a loader followed by a Hero.

It is the first state of the Hero.

Conceptual transition:

```text
BOOT
  ↓
PARAMETERS ENGAGE
  ↓
SEGMENTED RAIL FILLS
  ↓
SYSTEM READY
  ↓
startup geometry remains on screen
  ↓
modules translate / collapse / align
  ↓
calibration marks become interface structure
  ↓
identity copy resolves
  ↓
HOME IS NOW PRESENT
```

The machine resolves into the website.

### Startup semantic parameters

Possible parameters:

```text
IDENTITY
SYSTEMS
EVIDENCE
INTERFACE
```

No fabricated vehicle telemetry belongs here.

## 4. Hero v2

Hero responsibility remains capability-first, but capability presentation must not be a four-card startup grid.

The Hero needs to communicate:

- Eduardo Merino;
- Software Developer — Systems, Full Stack & Applied AI;
- “I turn messy operational problems into working software.”;
- ability to inspect systems;
- clear route to Systems and Contact.

### Composition direction

Use:

- controlled asymmetry;
- oversized editorial typography;
- technical registration marks tied to real layout;
- an active line/path inherited from startup;
- one dominant interaction rather than multiple boxed features;
- negative space as part of the composition.

Do not use:

- four equal capability cards;
- conventional SaaS hero right-column dashboard;
- decorative fake telemetry;
- a standalone loader that vanishes.

## 5. Home systems as moments, not cards

### AutoPulse

AutoPulse can be introduced as an oversized technical object crossing a viewport.

Possible ingredients:

- `01 / AUTOPULSE` as persistent system identity;
- `Capture → Persist → Recover → Analyze` as a traceable path;
- one real product/evidence artifact;
- one state/failure observation;
- one visible evidence entry point.

As the visitor progresses, the object can separate into layers rather than simply reveal a card.

### CV Engine

CV Engine must not reuse the AutoPulse composition.

Its system behavior is different:

```text
Career Evidence
    ↓
Job Snapshot
    ↓
Match Report
    ↓
Decision
    ↓
Application
```

The shared design language comes from motion, typography, materials, evidence, and notation — not a shared card template.

## 6. Mechanical intensity by surface

```text
STARTUP          moderate but abstract
HOME             low
SYSTEMS INDEX    low
AUTOPULSE        moderate / earned by domain
CV ENGINE        very low literal automotive language
EVIDENCE         almost none literal
NOTES            almost none literal
ABOUT            light personal mechanical moment
CONTACT          minimal
```

If a viewer describes the portfolio as “car-themed,” the design fails.

## 7. Visual concept render — approved role

A generated concept render produced during STEP 0.8 successfully demonstrated a stronger atmosphere than the first static Figma exploration.

What it proved visually:

- cinematic dark engineering atmosphere can work;
- dense technical framing can still feel premium;
- mechanical imagery can create identity;
- oversized system presentation is stronger than generic project cards;
- red accent can create hierarchy when controlled;
- a case/system can dominate the composition instead of being one card among many.

What it **does not** prove:

- factual copy;
- metrics;
- Eduardo's portrait/appearance;
- location;
- availability;
- AutoPulse production maturity;
- final component layout;
- final motion behavior;
- accessibility/performance readiness.

The render is a **visual reference artifact**, not the website specification.

## 8. Render elements to keep conceptually

- dark engineered environment;
- red signal accent;
- industrial/editorial tension;
- strong spatial hierarchy;
- technical annotations;
- oversized project/system moment;
- selective mechanical photography/imagery;
- clear conversion CTA;
- sense of inspection rather than browsing generic cards.

## 9. Render elements to reject or rewrite

The concept render contains invented or unsupported information and must not be copied as factual content.

Examples to reject unless independently proven:

- `50K+ lines of code`;
- `15+ projects delivered`;
- `5+ systems in production`;
- `3000+ hours of deep focus`;
- geographic `BASE` values not explicitly confirmed;
- generic `OPEN TO WORK` state unless intentionally published;
- production/impact claims stronger than evidence supports;
- generated portrait as Eduardo's identity.

## 11. Motion proof requirement

At least one important transition must exist as actual motion/prototype behavior before STEP 0.8 is accepted.

Minimum preferred proof:

```text
Startup → Home transformation
```

Secondary proof:

```text
AutoPulse system identity → Architecture / Evidence inspection
```

## 12. Acceptance tests

### 10-second comprehension

A visitor can identify Eduardo, his role, his value proposition, and where to inspect work.

### Trust

The portfolio feels appropriate for a serious client or technical reviewer.

### Evidence clarity

Claim, model, implementation, and evidence remain distinguishable.

### Distinctiveness

The experience is memorable without relying on automotive cosplay.

### Mechanical subtlety

The interface feels engineered, not themed.

### Conversion

The visitor can progress naturally toward contact.

## 13. Fail conditions

FAIL if:

> “Cool car website.”

FAIL if:

> “Another clean developer portfolio.”

PASS target:

> **“Serious systems-oriented software developer; unusually precise, crafted, and inspectable.”**

## 14. Current STEP 0.8 decision

The generated concept render gives enough confidence to move toward BUILD **only after** a final Build Contract removes invented claims, freezes screens/content/publicability, and converts the visual concept into executable slices.

The render is inspiration.
The build contract is authority.
