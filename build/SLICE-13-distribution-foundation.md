## Why this slice exists

STEP 1.1 closed portfolio construction. THE BUILD ROOM already has enough public, inspectable proof to support a recurring reputation system without adding more portfolio pages.

The next responsibility is therefore distribution:

```text
SYSTEM / EVIDENCE / NOTE
        ↓
PUBLIC ENGINEERING POST
        ↓
TARGETED PORTFOLIO ROUTE
        ↓
INSPECTION
        ↓
CONVERSATION
        ↓
LEARNING
```

The portfolio remains the source of truth. Distribution is downstream and cannot strengthen claims beyond the underlying evidence.

## Scope

This slice creates the operating foundation for the first LinkedIn distribution cycle.

It does **not**:

- redesign the production portfolio;
- change application source;
- add public routes;
- invent a direct email/contact channel;
- automate publication before the editorial contract is proven;
- claim platform reach or conversion outcomes before real posts exist.
